Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about squint surgery and strabismus treatment

Squint surgery (strabismus surgery) is a procedure to correct eye misalignment. It's needed when non-surgical treatments like glasses or prisms aren't effective. The surgery adjusts eye muscle tension to improve alignment and may enhance binocular vision.

Yes, squint surgery is highly successful in adults. Modern techniques achieve excellent cosmetic results and can often improve binocular vision. Age is not a barrier to successful treatment, and many adults achieve significant improvement in eye alignment.

Most patients can return to normal activities within 1-2 weeks. Complete healing takes 6-8 weeks. During recovery, you may experience mild discomfort, redness, and temporary double vision, which typically resolves within a few days to weeks.

Dr. Ernest combines 20+ years of experience with the latest surgical techniques. He offers personalized care, uses minimally invasive methods, and has successfully performed over 5000 squint surgeries with a 98% success rate.
