═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎉 CONGRATULATIONS! Your website is ready!

Follow these 5 simple steps to customize and launch:

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 1: VIEW THE WEBSITE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Double-click "index.html" to open in your web browser
2. Browse through all pages:
   - Homepage (index.html)
   - About Us (about.html)
   - Programs (programs.html)
   - Enrollment (enroll.html)
   - Contact (contact.html)
3. Test on your phone too!

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 2: UPDATE CONTACT INFO (15 minutes)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Open each HTML file in a text editor (Notepad, VS Code, etc.)
Use Find & Replace (Ctrl+H) to replace:

   [Address]           → Your street address
   [Postal Code]       → Your postal code
   (647) 289-6983      → Your phone number
   info@wonderkidsdaycare.ca → Your email
   [YEAR]              → Year you started

Do this for ALL 5 HTML files!

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 3: ADD YOUR LOGO & IMAGES (30 minutes)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Save your logo as: images/logo.png
2. Add these images to the images/ folder:
   - hero-image.jpg (homepage background)
   - infants-program.jpg
   - toddlers-program.jpg
   - preschool-program.jpg
   - staff-1.jpg through staff-4.jpg

See images/IMAGE_GUIDE.txt for details!

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 4: CUSTOMIZE COLORS (10 minutes)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Open: css/style.css
2. Find lines 7-20 (the :root section)
3. Change the color codes to match your brand:

   --primary: #FF9A8B;     ← Change this!
   --secondary: #A8E6CF;   ← And this!

Use a color picker: https://coolors.co

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 5: LAUNCH YOUR SITE (30 minutes)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Free hosting options:

OPTION A - NETLIFY (Recommended)
1. Go to netlify.com
2. Sign up for free
3. Drag your entire project folder to Netlify
4. Done! Your site is live!

OPTION B - Your own web hosting
1. Upload all files via FTP
2. Done!
