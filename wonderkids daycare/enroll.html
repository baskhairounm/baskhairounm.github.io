Enrollment FAQs

Common questions about joining Wonderkids

How long is the waitlist?

Waitlist times vary by program and desired start date. Infant spots typically have the longest wait (3-6 months), while toddler and preschool programs may have immediate or short-term availability. We'll give you an estimate when we contact you.

Is there a fee to join the waitlist?

No, joining our waitlist is completely free with no obligation. Once a spot becomes available and you decide to enroll, there will be a registration fee.

Can I tour the facility before enrolling?

Absolutely! We encourage all families to tour our facility before making a decision. Tours are available Monday-Friday. Contact us to schedule a visit.

What documents do I need for enrollment?

You'll need your child's birth certificate, immunization records, health card information, and emergency contact details. We'll provide a complete checklist when you enroll.

Do you offer part-time care?

Yes, we offer both full-time and part-time care options depending on availability. Let us know your preferred schedule in the application form.

What are your fees?

Our fees vary by program and are competitive with licensed childcare in Milton. We'll discuss specific rates during your consultation. We also accept subsidies and can help with funding applications.
