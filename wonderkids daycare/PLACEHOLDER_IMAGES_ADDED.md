## 🔄 How to Replace with Real Photos

When you're ready to add your own photos:

1. **Take or gather your photos**
2. **Save them with the same filenames** (but .jpg or .png extension)

3. **Update the HTML files** to point to your new image files
4. **Or** just replace the SVG files directly with same filenames
