Our Programs

Age-appropriate learning experiences tailored to every developmental stage

At Wonderkids Daycare, we offer comprehensive programs designed to nurture your child's development at every age. Our play-based curriculum encourages exploration, creativity, and social-emotional growth in a safe and stimulating environment.

6 weeks - 18 months

Infant Program

Our infant program provides a warm, nurturing environment where your baby's individual needs are met with gentle care and attention. We follow each child's unique schedule while providing stimulating activities to support early development.

What We Offer:

  • Individualized care following your baby's feeding and sleeping schedule
  • Safe, clean, and cozy environment designed for infants
  • Sensory exploration through age-appropriate toys and activities
  • Daily communication about your baby's day (feedings, naps, diaper changes)
  • Tummy time and activities to encourage motor skill development
  • Soothing routines and one-on-one bonding time
  • Music, stories, and gentle play to stimulate cognitive development
  • Secure outdoor time in strollers or safe play areas (weather permitting)
Ratio: Low infant-to-educator ratio for personalized attention
Hours: Monday - Friday, 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM

18 months - 3 years

Toddler Program

Our toddler program is designed to support your child's growing independence and curiosity. Through structured play and exploration, toddlers develop important social skills, language abilities, and self-confidence.

What We Offer:

  • Play-based learning that encourages exploration and discovery
  • Activities to develop fine and gross motor skills
  • Language development through stories, songs, and conversation
  • Social and emotional skill building through group play
  • Creative arts and crafts activities
  • Daily outdoor play in a secure playground
  • Introduction to routines and self-help skills (toileting, hand washing)
  • Healthy, nutritious meals and snacks
  • Sensory play and hands-on learning experiences
Ratio: Optimal toddler-to-educator ratio
Hours: Monday - Friday, 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM
Enroll Now
3 - 5 years

Preschool Program

Our preschool program prepares children for kindergarten and beyond. Through a balanced mix of structured learning and creative play, children develop the academic, social, and emotional skills they need to succeed.

What We Offer:

  • Kindergarten readiness activities (pre-reading, pre-math, writing skills)
  • STEM exploration and hands-on science activities
  • Creative expression through art, music, and dramatic play
  • Social skills development and conflict resolution
  • Circle time with group discussions and sharing
  • Physical education and outdoor active play
  • Character building and emotional intelligence
  • Problem-solving and critical thinking activities
  • Field trips and special events (age-appropriate)
  • Healthy meals and snacks that children help prepare
Ratio: Small group sizes for optimal learning
Hours: Monday - Friday, 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM
