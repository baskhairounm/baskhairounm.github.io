Quick Answers

Common questions we receive

How do I schedule a tour?

You can schedule a tour by calling us at (647) 289-6983, emailing info@wonderkidsdaycare.ca, or filling out the contact form above. Tours are available Monday through Friday during operating hours.

Do you have spaces available?

Space availability varies by program and age group. Please contact us directly or complete a waitlist application to learn about current openings.

Where can I park for drop-off?

We have on-site parking available for families. There are designated drop-off spots near the entrance for quick and convenient access.

How can I apply for childcare subsidy?

We accept childcare subsidies through Halton Region. Our staff can provide guidance on the application process and required documentation. Contact us to learn more.
